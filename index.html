🌟 What is VRMacroPad?

VRMacroPad is a native Windows application that brings the convenience of a physical macro deck like the Elgato Stream Deck directly into your virtual reality environment or as a simple desktop window. Instead of fumbling with keyboards or taking off your headset, you can press large, colorful buttons floating in front of you—or on your wrist—to trigger anything on your PC.

Each button is fully customizable with your own color, label, and action. You can also place live mini-screens next to your buttons to keep an eye on important information while you're immersed in VR, like your OBS recording status, Twitch chat metrics, or your current Spotify track.

It's built for creators, streamers, and power users who want to stay in flow.

🚀 Getting Started - Complete Beginner's Guide

Follow these simple steps to get VRMacroPad running on your Windows PC. No programming experience needed!

📋 What You'll Need

Before you begin, ensure your system meets these basic requirements:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • RAM: 8 GB or more
  • Graphics: Compatible with SteamVR if you plan to use the overlay mode
  • Storage: 200 MB of free space
  • An internet connection for initial setup and optional Twitch integration

🛠️ Installation Steps

  1. Download the application: Visit this link to download the application. This link takes you to the official GitHub page where you'll find the latest version. Look for the "Releases" section on the left side or scroll down to find the latest release. Download the setup file (usually named VRMacroPad-Setup.exe).
  2. Run the installer: Once the download finishes, double-click on the downloaded file. Windows may ask for permission to make changes; click "Yes" to proceed.
  3. Follow the setup wizard: The installer will open. Just click "Next" a few times, choose where you want to install it (default is fine), and click "Install." This takes less than a minute.
  4. Launch the application: After the installation completes, tick the box "Launch VRMacroPad" and click "Finish." The app will start automatically. If you don't tick it, you can find VRMacroPad in your Start Menu or desktop shortcut.
  5. Start SteamVR (optional): If you want the overlay in VR, start SteamVR with your headset. VRMacroPad will detect it and show your button panel floating in your virtual space. If you just want a desktop window, that's the default behavior—simply start it without VR.
💡 First-time tip: Right-click anywhere on the overlay to open the editor. From there, you can add, remove, or reconfigure your buttons, change colors, and assign actions. The dashboard editor lets you drag and drop new buttons to create your perfect layout.

🛠️ Troubleshooting & Getting Help

Encountering an issue? Here are common fixes:

  • Overlay not showing: Make sure SteamVR is completely running before starting VRMacroPad. Check that the "Overlay Mode" is enabled in VRMacroPad settings.
  • Buttons not responding: Right-click to reopen the editor, verify that your button actions are correctly configured. Ensure the target apps (OBS, Voicemeeter) are running.
  • Performance issues: Reduce the number of live screens or lower their refresh rate in the performance settings.
  • More help: Visit the GitHub repository and check the Issues tab for similar problems. You can also create a new issue— the community is friendly and helpful.
